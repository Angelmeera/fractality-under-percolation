# Figures

The figures of the manuscript and its Supplementary Material (SM), each as a 600-dpi
PNG and a vector EPS drawn at the printed width of 5.0 in (see `src/paper_style.py`).
They are regenerated by the scripts in `src/` and `revision/` from the files in
`results/` and `revision/results/`. File names predate the paper's numbering, so the
table below gives the number of each figure in the **revised** manuscript.

| file | revised paper | subject | drawn by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fig1_pc_dB` | Fig. 1 | susceptibility and `d_B(p)` through the percolation transition | `src/make_fig1_fig4.py` |
| `fig2_mass_law_iv` | Fig. 2 | `β` and `α` under damage: OLS on the damaged degree against IV with the pre-damage degree, three box-mass conventions, per level at `t = 7` | `revision/make_figs_rev.py` |
| `fig3_restoration` | Fig. 3 | the giant component level by level at `t = 7`: hub-degree thinning, surviving mass, chemical stretch | `revision/make_figs_rev.py` |
| `fig8_grid_and_size` | SM Fig. S1 | box-size grid selection, the parity effect on the WWW, DBLP `d_B` against backbone size | `src/make_snap_figs.py` |
| `fig4_clustering` | SM Fig. S2 | clustered `G^A` versus triangle-free `G^B` | `src/make_fig1_fig4.py` |
| `fig6_dblp` | SM Fig. S3 | percolation of the DBLP `w ≥ 60` backbone | `src/make_fig6_dblp.py` |
| `fig7_fractality` | SM Fig. S4 | the fractality test on fractal networks and controls | `src/make_snap_figs.py` |
| `fig9_fss` | SM Fig. S5 | finite-size scaling for the percolation critical exponents | `src/make_fss_fig.py` |
| `fig3_alpha_beta_corrected` | — | **submitted** Fig. 2: `α(p)`, `β(p)` under three conventions, OLS only; superseded by `fig2_mass_law_iv` | `src/make_fig3_conventions.py` |
| `fig5_convergence` | — | closure residual and simulated `p_c` against system size (not in the manuscript; its numbers are in Table 3 and Sec. 4.1) | `src/make_fig1_convergence.py` |

`fig2_mass_law_iv` (Fig. 2) is the one to look at first. The fall of `β` that the usual
fit reports depends on the box-mass convention, and the figure shows why. Against the
hubs' structural (pre-damage) degree, the within-level slope stays close to 1 under all
three conventions.

`fig9_fss` was redrawn for the revision. Its axis labels now use `β_perc` and
`γ_perc` for the percolation exponents, and panel (a)'s lower axis limit was changed;
the data are unchanged.
